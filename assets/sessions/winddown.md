# Wind-down

Short, for "just something short" at night - breath, then letting go of tomorrow.

A slow breath in. [pause 2s] And a longer one out. [pause 3s] One more, as slow as you
can make it. [pause 4s]

Your plan for tomorrow is already saved. There's nothing in it you need to hold onto
right now - I have it. [pause 5s]

Nothing to solve tonight. [pause 4s]

[pause 8s]
