# Breathing reset

About two minutes: a couple of double-inhale, long-exhale sighs, then slow breathing.

Let's reset things. Breathe in through your nose - and on top of that breath, take one
more small sip of air. [pause 1s] Now let it all out slowly through your mouth, longer
than the breath in. [pause 4s]

Once more. In through the nose, and a second small sip on top. [pause 1s] And out,
slow and long. [pause 4s]

One more like that. [pause 1s] And out. [pause 5s]

Now just breathe normally, a little slower than usual. In. [pause 2s] Out, longer.
[pause 3s] In. [pause 2s] Out, longer. [pause 4s]

That's it. However stressed you were a minute ago, you're carrying slightly less of it
now. [pause 3s]
