# Outdoor morning meditation

Played while the gate is running - eyes open, notice light, sounds, breath.

Eyes open for this one. You're already outside, which is most of the work done. [pause
3s]

Notice the light for a moment - not staring at it, just the brightness of it, wherever
you're looking. [pause 5s]

Notice one sound you can hear right now that you weren't paying attention to a moment
ago. [pause 5s]

And notice your breath, without changing it. Just in, and out. [pause 5s]

That's really all there is to this one. Light, sound, breath, while the light does its
work. [pause 6s]

[pause 8s]
