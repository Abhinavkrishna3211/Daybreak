# NSDR: long

Settle in, slow breathing with long exhales, a full body scan, then effortless awareness.
Written for Jarvis to read aloud; rendered once with Piper by scripts/render_sessions.py.
`[pause Ns]` becomes N seconds of silence, not a spoken instruction.

There's nowhere to be for the next while, and nothing to get right. Let your eyes close,
or just soften them, whichever you'd rather. [pause 4s]

Take a slow breath in, and let it go longer than feels natural. Again - in, and a long,
slow way out. [pause 3s] One more like that. In. And out, longer than you think you need
to. [pause 5s]

Now just let your breathing find its own pace. You don't have to do anything with it.
[pause 4s]

Bring your attention to your feet. Not moving them, just noticing them - the weight of
them, wherever they are. [pause 4s] Let that attention drift up through your ankles, your
calves. [pause 4s] Up through your knees, and your thighs. [pause 4s] Notice your hips,
your lower back, resting. [pause 4s]

Now your stomach, rising and falling on its own. [pause 3s] Your chest, doing the same.
[pause 3s] Your shoulders - let them drop, even a little further than you think they can.
[pause 4s]

Down through your arms, to your hands. Just noticing them. [pause 4s]

And up through your neck, your jaw - let it hang loose - and your face. Nothing to hold
up here either. [pause 5s]

For a while now, there's nothing to do but notice. Sounds in the room, if there are any.
The feeling of the air. Nothing to change about any of it. [pause 8s]

[pause 10s]

If your mind has wandered off somewhere, that's exactly what minds do. No need to pull it
back sharply - just notice, and let it settle again. [pause 8s]

[pause 10s]

Whenever you're ready, there's no hurry about it, you can let this drift into sleep, or
just rest here as long as you like. [pause 6s]
