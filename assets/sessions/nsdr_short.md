# NSDR: short

The same arc as the long version, condensed to about ten minutes.

Let your eyes close, or just soften them. Nowhere to be right now. [pause 3s]

A slow breath in, and a longer way out. [pause 2s] Once more - in, and let it go slower
than feels natural. [pause 4s] Now just let it find its own rhythm. [pause 3s]

Notice your feet, your legs, settling. [pause 4s] Your stomach and chest, moving on their
own. [pause 3s] Your shoulders, dropping a little further. [pause 3s] Your face, nothing
held up. [pause 4s]

Just notice, for a while. Whatever sounds are around you. The weight of you, resting.
[pause 8s]

[pause 8s]

If your mind has wandered, that's fine - let it settle back without any effort. [pause
6s]

Whenever you're ready, drift into sleep, or just stay resting here. [pause 5s]
