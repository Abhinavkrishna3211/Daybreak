# Calm-down

About five minutes: grounding, then slow breathing.

Let's slow this down together. Without moving your head, find five things you can see
right now. [pause 6s] Don't name them aloud, just look at each one for a second. [pause
6s]

Now notice four things you can hear. [pause 6s]

And three things you can feel - the chair, the floor, the air on your skin. [pause 6s]

Good. Now a slow breath in. [pause 2s] And a longer one out. [pause 3s] Again - in.
[pause 2s] Out, slower. [pause 4s]

Whatever it was, it's still there if it needs dealing with. It just doesn't need dealing
with in the next five minutes. [pause 4s]

One more slow breath. [pause 2s] And out. [pause 4s]
